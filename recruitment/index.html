Automations for the admin that slows your consultants down

I've spent two years in recruiting, and I build small automations for independent agencies: applications logged in your ATS without retyping, interviews booked without the back-and-forth, and documents and timesheets chased for you, with your team approving anything that goes to a candidate or client.

Where the week goes

Applications arrive from Indeed, Reed, CV-Library and your own website, and someone uploads or retypes each one into the ATS. A few never make it in.

Booking one interview takes five emails between the candidate, the client and the consultant, and moving it takes five more.

Right-to-work documents, references and certificates get chased by hand, and a placement waits on the last one. On contract desks, Friday goes on chasing timesheets.

What I build

Applications that land in your ATS

Each application from the job boards and your site is added to your ATS with the CV attached and matched to the candidate's existing record if they've applied before. Anything that fails to arrive is flagged rather than lost. It's the same idea as a watchdog I built for letting agents, which checks that every portal enquiry reached the CRM: you can try that one here.

Interviews booked without the back-and-forth

Once the client has given you their slots, the candidate picks one that also suits the consultant, everyone gets a confirmation and a reminder, and the calendars update. Anything the rules don't cover, like a client asking to move the interview, goes straight to your consultant.

Documents and timesheets chased for you

Polite reminders go out on a schedule you set until the right-to-work document, reference or timesheet arrives, and stop the moment it does. Your consultant can see where each placement stands without opening their inbox.

A tidier database

Possible duplicate candidate records are flagged for your team to approve before anything is merged, and adverts for roles you've closed are flagged so they come down.

Anything sent to a candidate or client uses wording you've approved, and every draft is checked against your rules before it goes out, for example never mentioning pay rates, never confirming an offer, and passing anything unusual to the consultant. This demo shows how the checks work. You can keep it in draft-only mode, where your team sends everything themselves, for as long as you like.
